Companies have reconciliation systems. Consumers have memory and screenshots.

I built **RealityCheck** for Google's **All Things Agentic Hackathon**: an autonomous Gemini + Google ADK agent that captures what you were promised, observes what happened later, computes the “reality diff,” and - with permission - pursues correction until the outcome is verified.

The demo catches a ₹350 ISP installation fee that was explicitly promised free, prepares the evidence-backed correction, monitors the provider's 48-hour credit promise, and closes only after the credit actually appears.

Built with Gemini 3.5 Flash, Google ADK, Cloud Run, Firestore, Pub/Sub, and Cloud Scheduler.

Reality should match the agreement.
